Expository note · 5 pages

The Quadratic Lorentzian Cone: A Self-Contained Proof of the Closure Property

An independently developed proof using Perron–Frobenius theory, explicit perturbations, and Schur complements, written during a reading group on Brändén and Huh’s Lorentzian Polynomials under the guidance of Botong Wang.

A shorter argument, shared by Petter Brändén in a private conversation. For a nonzero entrywise nonnegative symmetric matrix A with at most one positive eigenvalue, let J be the all-ones matrix and set A(s) = (I + sJ)A(I + sJ), s > 0. This invertible congruence preserves inertia by Sylvester’s law, while making every entry strictly positive; moreover, A(s) tends to A as s tends to zero. The zero matrix is approximated separately by εJ − ε2I. Brändén also explained the connection with the proof of Theorem 2.13.
